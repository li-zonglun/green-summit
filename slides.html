About the Summit

SEE the Green Future

An online summit by the Sino-European Exchange (SEE).

Chinese scholars pitch green-transition ideas to European experts who mentor, challenge, and guide them — building bridges between the two largest forces shaping the global green transition.

Event Structure

Four Phases to Impact (1/2)

01. Submission

📍 Online  |  ⏱ 2 Weeks
  • 3-min video presentation (clear, structured)
  • 1-page handout: problem, argument, strategy
  • Focus: green transition, China-Europe comparative lens

02. Evaluation & Selection

📍 Online  |  ⏱ 2 Weeks
  • Panel of 5–10 European mentors reviews submissions
  • Top 6 teams selected with detailed feedback
  • Each team assigned a European mentor

Event Structure

Four Phases to Impact (2/2)

03. The Online Summit

📍 Online (Zoom)  |  ⏱ 1 Day
  • Final presentations by selected teams
  • Q&A sessions with judges
  • Expert talks & panel discussions

04. Post-Summit Development

📍 Online  |  ⏱ ∞
  • Continue developing work with mentor
  • Further research opportunities
  • Ongoing academic & professional engagement

Participants

Two Delegations, One Vision

🇨🇳 Chinese Delegation

Idea Sharers + Observers

  • Selected through competition, receive mentorship, present at summit
  • Join keynotes, attend expert talks
  • University students & young scholars

🇪🇺 European Delegation

Mentors + Observers

  • Guide research, judge ideas, share expertise
  • Public officials, ESG consultants, researchers
  • Students from Sciences Po, Sorbonne, HEC Paris

The Organisation

The Sino-European Exchange

SEE is a student-led organisation dedicated to fostering meaningful dialogue between China and Europe.

  • Bridging Cultures: Genuine intellectual exchange breaking down stereotypes.
  • Developing Leaders: Nurturing young thinkers capable of navigating both policy landscapes.
  • Building Understanding: Mutual understanding is a necessity in an increasingly complex world.
