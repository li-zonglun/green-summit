Why This Summit

The Green Transition Demands New Voices

The global green transition sits at the intersection of economic transformation, policy-driven regulation, and geopolitical negotiation.

While top-level diplomatic channels exist between China and Europe, there is a critical gap — no platform connects the next generation of strategic talent with current European policy leaders.

This summit fills that void.

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

🎯

Our Mission

To facilitate profound intellectual and strategic exchange between China and Europe, empowering young talent with real-world sustainability and economic policy insights.

🔭

Our Vision

To establish an annual, globally recognized youth platform for sustainability and policy — the premier pipeline for future leaders entering international organizations, top consulting firms, and policy institutions.

The Three-Phase Journey

From Research to Reality

A selective, progressive competition culminating in an exclusive gathering in France.

01
Online

Policy Research Paper

Candidates submit a rigorous policy research essay on Environment & Global Policy, focusing on Europe-China relations and the green economy.

📝 Written essay submission
🌐 Open to all qualified candidates
✅ Top papers shortlisted for Phase 2
02
Suzhou, China

The Pitch Challenge

Shortlisted candidates transform their research into a consulting-style pitch deck, presented before leading Chinese and French professors.

🏛️ Renmin University Suzhou Campus
🎤 10-min presentation + 5-min Q&A
🏆 Winners advance to the European Summit
03
France — The Finale

The European Summit

Top teams travel to France to pitch their strategies to a board of esteemed European decision-makers, alongside keynote speeches and structured networking.

🇫🇷 High-level keynotes by European leaders
🤝 Networking with decision-makers & peers
🎓 Awards, mentorship & cross-cultural exchange
