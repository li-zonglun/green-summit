About the Summit

SEE the Green Future Summit

An online summit by the Sino-European Exchange (SEE). Chinese scholars pitch green-transition ideas. European experts mentor, challenge, and guide them.

🌍

Context

Green transition = economics + regulation + geopolitics. China and Europe are the two biggest players. Collaboration isn't optional.

🎯

Purpose

Connect young Chinese scholars with European policy leaders. Build a platform for early-stage intellectual exchange with a global outlook.

🤝

Engagement

Chinese students get international exposure. European professionals discover China's next generation of strategic thinkers.

Event Structure

Four Phases to Impact

Research, mentorship, and high-level exchange — all online.

01
Submission · 2 Weeks

Submission

🎬 3-min video presentation (clear, structured)
📄 1-page handout: problem, argument, strategy
🎯 Focus: green transition, China-Europe comparative lens
02
Evaluation · 2 Weeks

Evaluation & Selection

🏆 Panel of 5–10 European professors reviews submissions
💬 Top 6 teams selected with detailed feedback
👨‍🏫 Each team assigned a European mentor
03
Online Summit

The Online Summit

🎤 Final presentations by selected teams
❓ Q&A sessions with judges
🗣️ Expert talks & panel discussions
04
Post-Summit

Post-Summit Development

🔬 Continue developing work with mentor
📈 Further research opportunities
🌐 Ongoing academic & professional engagement
Participants

Chinese Delegation

Idea-Sharers & Learners
  • Idea-sharers: Selected through competition, receive European-professor mentorship, present at the summit
  • Learners: Join keynotes, hear speeches, engage in discussions
  • University students & young scholars seeking international exposure
  • Future professionals in consulting, policy & sustainability
🇪🇺

European Delegation

Mentors & Observers
  • Mentors & Speakers: Guide research, judge ideas, share expertise
  • Decision Makers: Public officials, ESG consultants, researchers, think-tank leaders
  • Student Observers: From Sciences Po, Sorbonne, HEC Paris — peer-to-peer exchange
