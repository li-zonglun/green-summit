About the Summit

SEE the Green Future Summit

“

An online summit by the Sino-European Exchange (SEE). Chinese scholars pitch green-transition ideas to European experts who mentor, challenge, and guide them — building bridges between the two largest forces shaping the global green transition.

”
🌍

Context

Green transition = economics + regulation + geopolitics. China and Europe are the two biggest players. Collaboration isn't optional.

🎯

Purpose

Connect young Chinese scholars with European policy leaders. Build a platform for early-stage intellectual exchange with a global outlook.

🤝

Engagement

Chinese students get international exposure. European professionals discover China's next generation of strategic thinkers.

Four Phases to Impact

Research, mentorship, and high-level exchange — all online.

01
Submission

The Organisation

The Sino-European Exchange

Our Mission

SEE (the Sino-European Exchange) is a student-led organisation dedicated to fostering meaningful dialogue between China and Europe. We believe that the next generation of leaders must be equipped with cross-cultural competence to navigate an increasingly interconnected world.

🌍

Bridging Cultures

We create spaces where Chinese and European students and professionals engage in genuine intellectual exchange, breaking down stereotypes and building lasting connections.

🌱

Developing Leaders

Through summits, research collaborations, and mentorship programmes, we nurture young thinkers who can operate confidently across both Chinese and European policy landscapes.

🤝

Building Understanding

In a world of increasing complexity between China and Europe, mutual understanding isn't a luxury — it's a necessity. SEE provides the platform to make it happen.
